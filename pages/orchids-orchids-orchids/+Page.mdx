**― English version ―**

The German Botanical Art Association (VBKD) warmly invites you to the upcoming exhibition "Orchids, Orchids, Orchids…" at the Dresden Trade Fairgrounds.

Orchids are among the most beautiful plants on our planet, with well over 2,500 species. While many different species grow wild in Germany, others are cultivated in garden centers and botanical gardens. Some are exotic species native to rainforests, jungles, and high mountain plateaus; others are found in colder regions of the earth.

The exhibition presents orchids painted using watercolor, colored pencils, graphite pencils, as well as ink and oil. The depicted orchids originate from various parts of the world.

As part of the conference, Audrey Reilly (VBKD President) and Esmee Winkel (The Netherlands) will hold a joint presentation on the topic "The Art of Painting Orchids." The artworks will be judged by a six-member expert jury across several categories, and visitors can also vote in a People's Choice Award.
